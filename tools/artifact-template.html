What this build is for

Ground tilt
Still at its guessed value. Blocks all vehicle art and track authoring — sprites have to be pre-rendered at whatever angle this lands on, so changing it later throws away art rather than code. Drag it and watch the grid.
Yaw rate
How fast the camera swings round to follow travel direction. The lag is deliberate, but it stacks with slip: a true 43° drift currently reads as 64° sideways. Thrilling or disorienting is your call.
Drift feel
Measured targets already pass — drifting clears a 90° corner in 40 ticks against 55 for steering. What a machine can't judge is whether it's satisfying, and whether a first-timer can hold a slide without spinning.

Deliberately absent

No art, sound, menus, progression or items. This is still a test arena rather than a designed track — a straight, a sweeper, a hairpin, a chicane and one ramp, chosen for corner variety and nothing else.

Opponents share the player's physics exactly. They produce the same inputs a keyboard does and run through the same controller, so they cannot do anything your car can't.

Every slider in the panel is live. Show tuning opens the current values as pasteable JavaScript.
